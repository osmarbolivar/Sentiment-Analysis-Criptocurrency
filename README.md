# Sentiment, Cryptocurrency and Inflation: A Transmission Channel in Bolivia

**[Read the full paper (PDF)](https://osmarbolivar.github.io/docs/papers_docs/WP_Crypto_Sentiment_Inflation.pdf)**

This repository contains the code accompanying the paper:
**"Sentiment, Cryptocurrency and Inflation: A Transmission Channel in Bolivia"**.

## Overview

This study investigates the dynamic interplay between economic uncertainty sentiment, the parallel cryptocurrency-based exchange rate, and inflation in Bolivia, using high-frequency data and advanced econometric modeling. In response to heightened macroeconomic volatility since early 2023, Bolivia has experienced increasing pressure on its currency peg, resulting in the widespread adoption of stablecoins—particularly USDT—as alternative stores of value.

Employing a dual methodological framework—comprising a calibrated Dynamic Stochastic General Equilibrium (DSGE) model and a Bayesian Structural Vector Autoregression (BSVAR)—the research identifies a transmission mechanism in which heightened uncertainty sentiment leads to depreciation of the parallel (digital) BOB/USDT exchange rate, amplifying inflationary pressures. Empirical impulse-response analysis, based on daily data from January 2023 to April 2025, confirms the model's predictions, revealing significant and persistent inflation effects triggered by sentiment shocks.

The findings highlight the stabilizing role of monetary policy, even within Bolivia's constrained interest rate environment. This work provides critical insights for policymakers overseeing digital currency markets, underscoring the importance of real-time sentiment monitoring and proactive liquidity management to mitigate inflation risks.

## Contents

* All code required to reproduce the analyses and results from the paper
